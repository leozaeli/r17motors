01

Hero / anúncio principal

Preserve the exact car, paint color, wheels, badges and proportions. Create a cinematic luxury automotive editorial look: warm late-afternoon sunlight, understated modern architecture, deep soft shadows, black and champagne color palette, subtle film grain, refined contrast, realistic reflections, premium magazine photography, no text, no logos, no altered vehicle details.
02

Detalhe de acabamento

Preserve the exact vehicle details. Enhance this image as a high-end automotive detail editorial: tactile material definition, controlled specular highlights, rich blacks, warm neutral whites, sophisticated low-key lighting, natural depth of field, clean background, photorealistic, no text, no watermark, no modified badges or parts.
03

Feed de estoque

Keep the vehicle completely authentic and unchanged. Retouch this inventory photo into a discreet luxury dealership campaign image: remove visual clutter, balance exposure, soften distracting reflections, use a warm ivory and dark charcoal grade, elegant shadow falloff, premium automotive photography, authentic paint texture, no text, no artificial accessories, no change to wheels, plate or emblems.
04

Entrega / prova social

Preserve the people and vehicle identity. Grade this delivery moment with a timeless editorial mood: warm golden-hour light, subtle champagne highlights, deep elegant shadows, candid emotion, refined composition, high-end automotive lifestyle photography, realistic skin tones, no text, no logos, no changes to the car.
